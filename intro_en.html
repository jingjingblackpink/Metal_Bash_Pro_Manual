Metal Bath PRO Intelligent Constant Temperature Metal Bath

B5 PRO — a high-precision constant-temperature metal bath heating instrument for laboratories. It adopts PID intelligent temperature control and a modular design, providing a stable and reliable temperature environment for molecular biology experiments.

  • ●PID intelligent temperature control with ±0.1°C accuracy
  • ●High-efficiency heating module: from room temperature to 100°C in just a few minutes
  • ●Multiple heating modules available, compatible with 0.2mL ~ 50mL centrifuge tubes and test tubes
  • ●Built-in overheat protection and temperature-abnormality alarm for safe and reliable experiments
  • ●LED digital display with intuitive keypad operation; preset temperature and timer programs
  • ●Suitable for sample incubation, enzyme reactions, DNA denaturation, serum inactivation, etc.
